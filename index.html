ABOUT

A little about me.

I’m a final-year B.Tech Mechanical Engineering student at IIT Guwahati, with a minor in Product Design. My background taught me to think in systems; product work taught me to start with people.

Over the last few years, I’ve worked across product strategy, analytics, AI/ML and business problem-solving — from studying markets and user behaviour to building data-backed recommendations and shaping product concepts. I enjoy problems where the answer isn’t obvious and the path to it requires both structured thinking and curiosity.

What ties my work together is a simple approach: understand the user, find the signal, frame the problem clearly, and turn the insight into something actionable. I’m particularly drawn to opportunities at the intersection of product, strategy, data and technology.
